Why agencies cost so much

Swiss web agencies charge CHF 120 to 250 an hour. At CHF 150 an hour, a website for CHF 9’000 is 60 hours of work.

Those hours hold project management, meetings, drafts made by hand, copy, programming and testing. Then there’s an office and a team that want paying every month. That’s how the model is built, and for large projects it has its place.

For a painter’s website it costs too much.

Why it costs less with me

AI writes the first draft of code, copy and ads in minutes. I check, correct and test every part myself. The hours that used to go into the draft are gone.

I work alone, from Schlieren. There’s no project manager, no office on Bahnhofstrasse and no meeting you help pay for.

What you don’t get in return: a team that carries ten projects at once. I only take on as many jobs as I can check myself.

What the price doesn’t include

  • Ad budget: you pay it straight to Google and Meta, with no markup.
  • WhatsApp fees from Meta for the AI assistant: the first 1’000 replies a month are free. After that, and for reminders sent more than 24 hours later, Meta charges CHF 0.02 to 0.07 a message. I pass that on with no markup.
  • Hosting and domain from year two: CHF 25 a month, or you move to a provider of your choice.
  • App store fees: Apple charges USD 99 a year, Google a one-off USD 25.
  • Servers and maintenance for an app after launch: a separate line in the quote.
  • Photos by a photographer and translations into further languages.

Payment

Half on order, half at launch. Monthly services can be cancelled to the end of any month. Code, domain and logins are yours after the final payment.
